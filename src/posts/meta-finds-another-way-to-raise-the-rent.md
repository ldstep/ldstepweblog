October 20, 2026

Meta is now charging to post links. Business and creator accounts get two free links a month. After that, they need a paid [Meta One subscription](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/), with tiers that run up to $499 a month per account.

Creators and small business owners are upset, and I get it. They built their audiences organically, and now they're paying to send people to their own websites.

This is a reminder of what happens when you don’t own your infrastructure. If you build on Meta’s platform, you’re renting, and the landlord just raised the rent. It' another money grab from a company you can't trust. 

**The open web is still there.**
