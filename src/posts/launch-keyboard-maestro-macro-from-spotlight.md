I've been experimenting with Spotlight's Siri AI integration since updating to macOS 27. I wanted to launch a Keyboard Maestro macro from Spotlight, but selecting the macro opened it in the editor instead.

So I asked Siri AI how I might do that and it suggested exporting the macro as a [trigger file](https://wiki.keyboardmaestro.com/trigger/Trigger_File). Once Spotlight indexes the file, selecting it runs the macro.
