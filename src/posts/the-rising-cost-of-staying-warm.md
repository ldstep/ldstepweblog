October 29, 2026

It's fall here in New Jersey, which means cold mornings and the heater running. We've already seen temperatures in the high 30s a few times. With colder weather on the way, we topped off our heating oil today.

We took delivery of 150 gallons for $869.85. Back in December 2025, the same amount cost only $466.35, or $3.109 per gallon. That's an increase of $2.69 per gallon—an 86% jump in less than a year!

Fortunately, we are in a position to absorb this increase. This will be a difficult winter for those who are barely getting by.
