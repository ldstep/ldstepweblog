November 26, 2026  
  
The timestamps atop notes in Apple Notes are clickable, to toggle between edited and created dates. When you first display a note, the timestamp at the top looks like this:  
  
```
November 26, 2026 at 1:00 pm
```
  
That’s the edited date (which I really want to call “modified”) — but it doesn’t say so. It just shows the date. When you click it, it changes to:  
  
```
Created: October 31, 2026 at 4:30 pm
```
  
Click again and you get:  
  
```
Edited: November 2, 2026 at 1:00 pm
```
  
If you continue clicking, it keeps toggling between the timestamps with the Created: and Edited: prefix labels.
