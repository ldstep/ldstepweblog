August 23, 2026

> Anyway, what I really want to remark on here is the idea of using AI to _brainstorm_. In the past, I may have written this off as well. But actually, with the rise of the [very good AI voice technology](https://spyglass.org/vocal-computing-ai/) — [namely, GPT-Voice](https://spyglass.org/gpt-live/) — I actually have found going back and forth with AI quite useful in helping to think through some ideas. And actually, it’s less about the content coming _from_ the AI, it’s just the fact that I'm having a conversation and it’s drawing ideas out of _my_ brain.
> 
> I’ve long found that some of my best ideas to write about come out of conversations. And while it’s obviously great to have those with _people_, people aren’t always immediately available to have a conversation with you. And again, if I’m being honest, it’s less about what the other person is saying and more that the chat is fleshing out my own ideas. In a way, I’m sort of “using” the human on the other end. Why not use an AI for that?
> 
> Again, I’ve found it quite interesting these past few weeks. These conversations with AI have spurred some ideas in my brain that I’m not sure I would otherwise have had. And to me, it's the actual _speaking_ that's key to that. Saying things out loud forces your mind into places – at least it does for me.

[M.G. Siegler](https://spyglass.org/ai-writing/)
