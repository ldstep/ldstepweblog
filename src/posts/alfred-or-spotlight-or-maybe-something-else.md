November 23, 2026

As a longtime Alfred user anticipating the release of Alfred 6, I decided to experiment with Spotlight to see if it could replace what I do in Alfred.

After a couple of weeks, I realized I wasn't missing anything in Alfred. I did have to create a couple of Keyboard Maestro macros to handle a few things, but that wasn't a big deal. I do find navigating Spotlight to be kinda clunky, though.

Also, after reviewing what's new in Alfred 6, I've concluded there's nothing there that I need. No reason to upgrade.

In the meantime, I [discovered](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/alfred-6-released/48418/12?u) [Tinycast](https://tinycast.dev/), a free alternative to Raycast that I'm taking a hard look at.
