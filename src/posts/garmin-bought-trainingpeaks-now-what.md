July 24, 2026

Garmin [announced](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/newsroom/press-release/corporate/garmin-acquires-trainingpeaks-and-trainheroic-leading-endurance-and-strength-training-platforms-for-athletes-and-coaches/) this week that it's acquired [TrainingPeaks](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/) and TrainHeroic. Garmin hasn't said much else. The press release is thin on details, so I've got some thoughts about what this could mean.

I started using TrainingPeaks back in 2006, after getting recruited to race on a competitive men's cycling team. My coach at the time was [Andy Applegate](https://trainright.com/coaches/andy-applegate/), a CTS coach who built all my workouts in TrainingPeaks. I stopped using it once I stopped racing, but my training history, race notes, results, and coaching plans are all still sitting in there. Years of it.

![](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/ldstep/my-blog-images/main/IMG_0220.PNG?width=800&format=webp)

**Worst case:** Garmin absorbs TrainingPeaks' features and paywalls them. Existing users lose access to that history — workout data, race notes, coaching plans, all of it — unless they pay up or migrate somewhere else in time.

That's the scenario that worries me. TrainingPeaks has been the de facto platform for coaches and athletes for a couple decades now. A lot of people have exactly what I have sitting in there: a long, unglamorous record of the work someone else designed for them. I'd hate to lose mine.
